A symptom is a starting point, not a diagnosis

Loss of power, flickering, recurring protective-device trips or a damaged device can have different causes. The useful information is what happened, where and when, not a guess made from equipment age. A Licensed Electrical Contractor should assess the installation within a defined diagnostic scope and explain the identified cause before repair work is approved.

Prepare the scope for your Bolton property

Write down the appliances that were running, the labelled breaker that tripped and the rooms that went dark. Leave the panel cover closed and avoid resetting a breaker that keeps tripping.

Ask for the fault to be assessed before walls or ceilings are closed, and for a note on whether it involves circuits that the renovation will extend or reuse.

What should the assessment establish?

What makes a breaker trip in a Bolton home when certain appliances run together? The report identifies the cause within a stated scope instead of blaming the appliance, and says whether the trips trace to one circuit, a connected device or a shared load. Where finished surfaces hide the cable, which parts would need opening? The contractor names each one. Who arranges any ESA notification? That name is in the scope too. Any repair should be approved only once the cause is explained, and kept separate from later renovation work.

Ontario electrical safety

ESA says almost all electrical work needs a notification of work. If you hire someone for home electrical work, use a Licensed Electrical Contractor. Agree who files the notification and request the Certificate of Acceptance after ESA review.

Read the current ESA notification and inspection guidance, contractor hiring guidance. You can verify an electrical contractor with ESA before agreeing to work.

Questions before you agree to work

Does a breaker that trips with two appliances mean the circuit is faulty?

Not necessarily. The two devices may share one circuit that is loaded by both, or something else may be involved. Only a contractor's assessment can tell which; until then, avoid running them together.

Should a tripping breaker be sorted out before the walls are finished?

Usually, an unexplained trip is better assessed while the cable can still be reached. If the cause is found after finishes go on, reaching it may mean opening surfaces that were just completed.

Useful project details

  • Equipment operating at the time

  • Owner or manager who can authorize access and repairs

Only photograph labels and locations that you can see safely. Do not remove electrical covers or approach damaged equipment to gather information.
