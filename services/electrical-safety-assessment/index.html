Electrical safety assessment in Bolton.

Before a renovation closes in the walls, a Bolton homeowner can ask for an assessment of the existing wiring that may stay. The request should state which rooms and equipment are included and what written result is expected.

A useful assessment has a stated purpose and boundary

A condition concern, renovation decision or insurer request can each call for a different assessment. Agree which equipment and areas will be reviewed, whether testing is included and what written result will be provided. Concealed wiring and inaccessible equipment may limit conclusions. A general visit should not be represented as complete certification of everything in the property.

Prepare the scope for your Bolton property

Collect any earlier inspection reports, ESA paperwork and invoices for past electrical work, and list the rooms where walls or ceilings will be opened or refinished.

Ask the contractor which existing wiring and devices the visit will look at, what testing is included and which concealed parts it cannot judge, so you know what stays unknown.

What should the assessment establish?

The homeowner decides whether, and when, to approve the work proposed for a Bolton renovation, and the report gives the grounds. It records which areas and equipment were reviewed and which could not be reached; in a two-storey home with limited ceiling access, that may include wiring above finished ceilings that cannot be seen without an opening. Each existing item carries the contractor's finding: apparently suitable to remain, needing further assessment, or due for replacement in the new work. Limits on the conclusions, such as concealed wiring, must be recorded so a general visit is not mistaken for approval of everything.

Ontario electrical safety

ESA says almost all electrical work needs a notification of work. If you hire someone for home electrical work, use a Licensed Electrical Contractor. Agree who files the notification and request the Certificate of Acceptance after ESA review.

Read the current ESA notification and inspection guidance, contractor hiring guidance. You can verify an electrical contractor with ESA before agreeing to work.

Questions before you agree to work

How do I find out whether existing wiring can stay in a renovation?

Ask a Licensed Electrical Contractor to assess the parts that will be kept and to report what was and was not visible. Reusing wiring is the contractor's judgment, not something to decide from age or appearance.

Is an assessment visit the same as an inspection of the whole house?

No. An assessment covers only what was agreed, and concealed or blocked areas may be left out. Agree the rooms and equipment in advance and ask for a written result, so the limits are clear.
